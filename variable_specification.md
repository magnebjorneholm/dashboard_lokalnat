# Variable Specification & Canonical Naming

Generated by verification_script.py against 3 test companies (REL00001, REL00886, REL03035).

## Data Source Classification

| File | Classification | Reasoning |
|------|---------------|-----------|
| `capbase_a.parquet` | **PRIMARY** | Component-level capital base. Cannot be derived from other sources. ~510k rows, 148 companies. |
| `capcost_a.parquet` | **DERIVED** | Exact pre-computation of KENT category-level output. 100% match with KENT on all 3 test companies (280 rows, 0.00 tkr max difference). |
| `Data_modeller.xlsx` | **MIXED** | CAPEX/Avskrivning/Avkastning columns = derived from capbase_a via KENT (verified). OPEXp = partially derived from SDF (94/115 match without NeoÄndringar). CU/MW/NS/MWhl/MWhh = independent volume data. |
| `Löpande kostnader från SDF 2024-27.xlsx` | **PRIMARY** | Regulatory submission data. Contains per-year controllable costs (2018-2021), non-controllable costs, and revenue frame components. Cannot be derived. |
| `EIs_DEA.xlsx` | **DERIVED** | Ei's baseline DEA results. Effkrav_proc can be exactly replicated from potential + is_outlier (148/148 exact matches). |
| `all_adjust_vars.csv` | **INDEPENDENT** | Incentive adjustment variables (48 variables per company). Unique data not found elsewhere. |
| `adjustment_final (1).csv` | **INDEPENDENT** | Final adjustment variables. Unique data. |

## Group 1: Capital Cost Variables

### capital_cost_first_year

- **Definition**: Total capital cost (depreciation + return on assets) for the first year of regulation period (2024), summed across all asset categories
- **Unit**: tkr, 2022 price level
- **Time scope**: Annual (2024 = sum of H1 + H2, timecodes 229 + 230)
- **Formula**: `sum(dep_ord_229 + dep_tail_229 + return_ord_229 + return_tail_229 + dep_ord_230 + dep_tail_230 + return_ord_230 + return_tail_230)` per network, in tkr
- **Primary source**: capbase_a.parquet → KENT steps 5-8 → aggregate_to_network_level → calculate_capex_outputs
- **Also found in**: Data_modeller.xlsx col `CAPEX`, aliased as `Kapitalkostnad_2024` in both KENT output and DM
- **Verification**: KENT vs DM: 2/3 exact match (0.00 tkr diff), REL00886 diff = -354 tkr (-0.08%) — likely different capbase_a version
- **Can be derived from capbase_a**: Yes
- **Current names in codebase**: `CAPEX`, `Kapitalkostnad_2024`
- **Canonical name**: `capital_cost_2024`

### capital_cost_{year} (2025, 2026, 2027)

- **Definition**: Total capital cost for a given year
- **Unit**: tkr, 2022 price level
- **Time scope**: Annual (sum of H1 + H2 for that year)
- **Formula**: Same as capital_cost_2024 but for timecodes {year}H1 + {year}H2
- **Primary source**: capbase_a → KENT → calculate_capex_outputs
- **Current names in codebase**: `Kapitalkostnad_2025`, `Kapitalkostnad_2026`, `Kapitalkostnad_2027`
- **Canonical name**: `capital_cost_2025`, `capital_cost_2026`, `capital_cost_2027`

### capital_cost_period

- **Definition**: Total capital cost over the entire regulation period 2024-2027
- **Unit**: tkr, 2022 price level
- **Time scope**: Period sum (4 years = 8 half-years)
- **Formula**: `Kapitalkostnad_2024 + Kapitalkostnad_2025 + Kapitalkostnad_2026 + Kapitalkostnad_2027`
- **Primary source**: capbase_a → KENT → calculate_capex_outputs
- **Also found in**: SDF IR sheet col `Kapitalkostnad`
- **Verification**: KENT vs SDF IR: 2/3 exact match, REL00886 diff = -1,332 tkr (-0.08%)
- **Current names in codebase**: `Kapitalkostnad_Period`, `Kapitalkostnad_Total` (in revenue frame assembly)
- **Canonical name**: `capital_cost_period`

### depreciation_first_year

- **Also found in**: Data_modeller.xlsx col `Avskrivning`

- **Current names in codebase**: `Avskrivning_2024`, `Avskrivning`
- **Canonical name**: `depreciation_2024`

### depreciation_{year} (2025-2027) and depreciation_period

- **Canonical names**: `depreciation_2025`..`depreciation_2027`, `depreciation_period`

### return_on_assets_first_year

## Group 2: Controllable Cost Variables

### controllable_cost_average

- **Definition**: Average historical controllable costs (2018-2021), annual, in 2022 price level
- **Unit**: tkr/year, 2022 price level
- **Time scope**: 4-year average (2018-2021)
- **Formula**: Mean of [2018, 2019, 2020, 2021] "Summa påverkbara kostnader i 2022 års prisnivå" from SDF Controllable sheet
  - Each year: `(Påverkbara + Kapitalkostnad_ej_kapbas + NeonÄndring) × Prisindex`
- **Primary source**: SDF Controllable sheet col `Medelvärde 2018-2021 påverkbara kostnader` (col 123)
- **Also found in**: Data_modeller.xlsx col `OPEXp` (partially — see verification)
- **Verification**:
  - Without NeoÄndringar: 94/115 companies (82%) exact match with DM OPEXp
  - 21/115 companies without Neo have discrepancies (up to 16.5% = 7,864 tkr)
  - With NeoÄndringar: 0/33 match hypothesis OPEXp = Medelvärde + Neo/4
  - **Conclusion**: OPEXp in Data_modeller is NOT always identical to SDF Medelvärde. Discrepancies likely from additional regulatory adjustments not visible in the SDF sheet
- **Current names**: `OPEXp` (DM), `Paverkbara_Medelvarde` (from `get_controllable_from_sdf()`)
- **Canonical name**: `controllable_cost_average`

### neo_adjustments

- **Definition**: Additional costs from company claims (separerat yrkandet) for 2018-2021, added to controllable baseline
- **Unit**: tkr, 2022 price level, 4-year period sum
- **Time scope**: Period sum
- **Primary source**: SDF Controllable sheet col `NeonAndringar? Ändring där nätföretagets inte separerat yrkandet...` (col 124)
- **Companies affected**: 33/148 have non-zero values
- **Notable**: REL00886 has Neo = 73,097 tkr
- **Current names**: `Neonjusteringar`
- **Canonical name**: `neo_adjustments_period`

### controllable_cost_{year} (2024-2027)

- **Definition**: Controllable cost for a specific year, after efficiency deduction
- **Unit**: tkr, 2022 price level
- **Formula**: `Starting_value - Cumulative_deduction_t + Annual_adjustment` where
  - Starting_value = controllable_cost_average (OPEX method) or + capex/4 (TOTEX)
  - Annual_adjustment = neo_adjustments / 4
  - Deduction uses compound efficiency requirement
- **Primary source**: Computed by `calculate_controllable_with_eff_req()`
- **Current names**: `Paverkbara_2024`..`Paverkbara_2027`
- **Canonical names**: `controllable_cost_2024`..`controllable_cost_2027`

### controllable_cost_period

- **Definition**: Period sum of controllable costs 2024-2027 after efficiency deduction
- **Unit**: tkr, 2022 price level
- **Also found in**: SDF IR col `Påverkbara kostnader` (verified: this IS the revenue frame component)
- **Current names**: `Paverkbara_Periodsumma`, `Paverkbara_I_Intaktsram`
- **Canonical name**: `controllable_cost_period`

## Group 4: DEA and Efficiency Variables

### dea_efficiency

- **Definition**: DEA efficiency score (1.0 = fully efficient, < 1.0 = inefficient)
- **Unit**: ratio (dimensionless)
- **Primary source**: EIs_DEA.xlsx col `Effektivitet`
- **Note**: Outliers (3 companies) have `Effektivitet = OUTLIER` (parsed as None)
- **Current name**: `Effektivitet`
- **Canonical name**: `dea_efficiency`

### dea_super_efficiency

- **Definition**: DEA super-efficiency score (can exceed 1.0)
- **Unit**: ratio (dimensionless)
- **Primary source**: EIs_DEA.xlsx col `Supereffektivitet`
- **Current name**: `Supereffektivitet`
- **Canonical name**: `dea_super_efficiency`

### dea_potential

- **Definition**: Improvement potential = 1 - efficiency (for non-outliers)
- **Unit**: ratio (0-1)
- **Primary source**: EIs_DEA.xlsx col `potential`
- **Current name**: `potential`
- **Canonical name**: `dea_potential`

### is_outlier

- **Definition**: Whether the company is classified as a DEA outlier
- **Unit**: boolean
- **Primary source**: EIs_DEA.xlsx col `Effektivitet` == "OUTLIER"
- **Companies affected**: 3/148
- **Current name**: `is_outlier`
- **Canonical name**: `is_outlier`

### efficiency_requirement_annual

- **Definition**: Annual efficiency requirement (percentage deduction per year)
- **Unit**: ratio (e.g. 0.0182 = 1.82% per year)
- **Formula**:
  - Outliers: fixed `outlier_req` (default 1.0%)
  - Non-outliers: `truncate(potential, min=16.24%, max=30%) → * 0.50 * (4/8) → compound^(1/4) - 1`
- **Primary source**: EIs_DEA.xlsx col `Effkrav_proc`
- **Verification**: `calculate_eff_req_for_dataframe()` replicates EIs_DEA.xlsx values with 148/148 EXACT matches (max diff = 0.0)
- **Range**: 1.00% (outliers/min) – 1.82% (max), mean = 1.23%
- **Parameters**: truncation_max=30%, customer_sharing=50%, realization_time=8yr, supervision_period=4yr
- **Current names**: `Effkrav_proc`
- **Canonical name**: `efficiency_requirement_annual`

## Naming Convention Applied

```
{metric}_{scope}_{qualifier}

Metrics:     capital_cost, depreciation, return_on_assets, controllable_cost,
             non_controllable_cost, efficiency_requirement, revenue_frame,
             dea_efficiency, dea_potential, incentive_adjustment
Scopes:      2024, 2025, 2026, 2027, period (=sum 2024-2027),
             average (=mean over historical period), first_year, annual
Qualifiers:  ord (ordinarie), tail, total, by_category
```

## Key Findings

### 1. capbase_a.parquet is the single source of truth for capital costs
All capital cost values in Data_modeller and SDF IR can be derived from capbase_a via KENT. Small differences (< 0.1%) for some companies (notably REL00886) suggest slight version differences in the capbase_a used by Ei.

### 2. capcost_a.parquet is redundant
It is a byte-for-byte identical pre-computation of KENT's `aggregate_to_category_level()` output. 280/280 rows match with 0.00 tkr difference across all value columns.

### 3. Volume variables (CU, NS, MWhl, MWhh) are 4-year averages
Confirmed by checking `value × 4` gives integer for 100% of companies. MW is likely also a 4-year average (55% have integer × 4, rest may have non-integer annual values).

### 4. OPEXp has unexplained discrepancies
94/115 companies without NeoÄndringar have OPEXp (DM) = Paverkbara_Medelvarde (SDF) exactly. 21 companies differ (up to 16.5%). The pipeline uses SDF as the source via `get_controllable_from_sdf()`, but DM's OPEXp is used as DEA input. This means DEA inputs and controllable cost calculations may use slightly different values for some companies.

### 5. Efficiency requirement is fully replicable
`calculate_eff_req_for_dataframe()` exactly reproduces all 148 Effkrav_proc values from EIs_DEA.xlsx (max diff = 0.0).

### 6. Revenue frame formula verified
`Intäktsram = Påverkbara + Opåverkbara + Flex + Avbrott + Kapitalkostnad + Avdrag` verified with diff = 0 for all 3 test companies.
